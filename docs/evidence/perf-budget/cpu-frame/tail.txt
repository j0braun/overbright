i/ = the integrated GPU. 'fixed' = the bench's one tick per frame; 60 / 144 = NFSU2_BENCH_HZ (the window's schedule).
Scenes: drive = a career sprint with traffic and three rivals to its finish; racing = a career circuit with three
rivals and traffic to its finish; race = that circuit's start (1500 frames). orig = Original, high = the Enhanced level High.

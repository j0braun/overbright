Ambient Detail's props made in the menus, kept for the drive (docs/perf-budget.md, docs/ambient-detail.md "When the props are placed").

Method: `--bench transitions` (developer harness, headless, offscreen; NFSU2_BENCH_MENU_FIRST=1), one binary per arm
(base = the tree before, new = this change), interleaved (odd rounds base first, even rounds new first), 7 rounds per arm,
each lock hold under 10 minutes; own game cache, Mesa shader cache and XDG folders. Wait = from the call to its first frame
drawn and the GPU done (wall ms); main-thread CPU = the load + its first frame on the calling thread (ms), and of it the
first frame's. Median (min–max).

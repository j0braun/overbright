The waits log's cost (docs/developing/waits-log.md): the shipping game (no developer harness) without the lines (the tree
before them) and with them, each from its own target folder (md5 differ). Windowed on a private Xvfb in a bwrap
sandbox (no sound device, NFSU2_AUDIO_HOST=none, own data / cache / Mesa shader cache folders per arm, both warm),
integrated GPU, 1920x1080, Original (default options), a copy of a test profile. Each run: --no-boot, the menus
to the garage, Return to Explore Mode, free roam with Up held 16 s, the pause menu, the window closed.
(Measured with the part size then 1,048,576 frames and the load's set-up mark a few lines earlier; the
per-frame path is the same.) Measured window: 3 s after the drive's start to 1 s before its end.
  a = the main thread's CPU time (/proc schedstat, sampled every 4 ms) over the window / the frames in it
      (the F3 sampler's fps rows of NFSU2_PERF_CSV, the same in both arms)
  b = median over the window's sampler rows of the main thread's CPU % * 10 / fps
7 pairs, ABBA order, one series under the measuring lock; load average 10-30 (other builds and runs on the
machine), so the CPU times carry other threads' contention.

The hooks' own cost (qol::waits::Tracker::frame, release build, one thread, synthetic drive at ~150 fps of
frame times, a stable screen; a throwaway test, not kept): 15.5 / 11.3 / 10.6 ns a frame over three rounds of
5,000,000 frames (the part summaries every 1,048,576 frames included). The game's hook adds the screen's
discriminants and the mode test: a few ns more.
A drive's summary (summarize + the line), best of 5: 10,000 frames 0.048 ms, 100,000 frames 0.514 ms,
1,000,000 frames 4.731 ms (linear; hence the part size of 262,144 frames: about 1.2 ms, interpolated).
Memory: 4 bytes per counted drive frame, at most 1 MB (a part), freed at the drive's end; lines at most 20,000 a
run. The runs' peak resident memory (the sampler's rss_mb): without 875 MB median [842..933], with 889 [843..951]:
the spread (the traffic and encounter cars are drawn anew each run), not the lines (their frames here: 16 KB).

What the lines said in those runs (the free roam entry; warm, with the lines):
  from the key 68-428 ms, the load itself 23-64 ms (world and car 5-25, renderers and uploads 0-12, set-up 2-8,
  programs 12-31), world and car loaded ahead, 0-4 cache files missing (new traffic and encounter cars), first
  frame 27-382 ms; drive medians 15.7-19.8 ms on Xvfb (its present is a CPU copy: frame times there are not the
  game's, docs/notes/performance/budget.md).

Not trusted: the absolute CPU times (machine shared, load 10-30); Xvfb frame times and hitches.

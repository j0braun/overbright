The launcher by the mouse alone: what was measured (docs/launcher.md, "The mouse"). The browser's picture at 150 %: tests/launcher/state-browse-many-1366x768@150.png.

== Before the change (folder browser over a home folder of 26 folders)
X11 (Xvfb + xdotool), scale 1: three wheel notches over the list left it on its first 6 rows; a click
  on the row chosen when the browser opened ("..") went up at once.
X11, WINIT_X11_SCALE_FACTOR=2: clicks land on the boxes (Browse... opened the browser).
Wayland (private headless mutter at scale 1.5, pointer through RemoteDesktop): winit reports scale 1.5
  and the cursor in physical pixels; clicks on Browse..., list rows and Cancel land; a wheel notch
  (LineDelta) and 10 touchpad steps (PixelDelta) left the list unchanged.

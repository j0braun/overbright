# Neon underglow quality

At **Car Shadow / Neon Ultra** the neon underglow is drawn from a smoothed, higher-resolution version of the
game's own neon texture: the blocky steps and hard contours of the original's compressed texture are gone,
with the same footprint, colour, pulse and glow size.

| Row | Value | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Car Shadow / Neon** (the original's Customize Display row with Overbright's tiers above it) | **Ultra** | the smoothed neon texture |

Original, Low and High draw the original's neon exactly. A texture pack that replaces the neon texture is used
as it is.

# Enhanced Car Paint

Paint with more depth: a sharp clear-coat highlight, metallic flake, and pearl and two-tone paints that shift
colour at grazing angles. Each paint keeps its colour and brightness at normal viewing.

| Row (Advanced → Image Quality) | Values | options.txt |
|---|---|---|
| **Enhanced Car Paint** | Off / On | `enh.enhanced_paint 1` |

The paint type comes from the game's own paint data: gloss, metallic, pearl, the two-tone custom paints, and
chrome. Glass, plastics, rubber, lights and decals stay as in the original (choice: the paint pass leaves these materials alone). It works with or without Enhanced
Reflections, with MSAA and with LOD Fade. **Off** (the default) draws the original's paint.

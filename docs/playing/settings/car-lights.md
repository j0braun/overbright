# Next-Gen car lights

With **Headlight Beams** at **Next-Gen**, every car's headlights cast visible cones into the air, its lamps
get halos and lens streaks, and its tail and brake lights light the road behind it. Rain makes the air
thicker, so the beams show more. Which cars have lights, where the lamps are, their colour and when the brake
lights come on stay the original's.

| Row (Advanced → Lighting & Effects) | Values | options.txt |
|---|---|---|
| **Headlight Beams** | Off / On / **Next-Gen** | `enh.headlight_beams 0/1/2` |

Off and On are described on the [Lighting II](lighting2.md) page. No preset sets Next-Gen.

**Cost** at 3840 × 2160 with the Extreme preset on the reference desktop GPU: +0.5 ms in heavy rain, +0.85 ms
in a night race, +1.0 ms in free roam with traffic.
